What is a Wave?

The big idea behind everything you see below.

⚡

Energy Transfer

A wave transfers energy from one place to another without transporting matter along with it.

🔄

Particle Vibration

In a mechanical wave, particles vibrate around their equilibrium positions while the disturbance travels.

🚀

Wave Motion

The wave can move forward even though individual particles mainly oscillate around their original positions.

Three Important Wave Motions

Transverse, longitudinal, and surface waves.

01 • TRANSVERSE

↕️ Up & Down

Particles move perpendicular to the direction the wave travels.

Examples: waves on a rope, many water-wave models.
02 • LONGITUDINAL

↔️ Back & Forth

Particles move parallel to the direction the wave travels.

Key terms: compression + rarefaction.
03 • SURFACE

🌊 Mixed Motion

Particles at a surface can show a mixture of transverse and longitudinal motion.

Example: waves traveling across the surface of water.
